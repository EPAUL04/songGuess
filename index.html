Guess My Song


Welcome! This game selects a song from your spotify library, then gives you seven attempts to guess which song has been selected. Clues are given as follows:

  1. a playlist you've added the song to
  2. when the song was first added to your library
  3. the artist(s)
  4. the album
  5. no extra clues!
  6. the first letter of the song's title
  7. no extra clues!

To play, log in to your Spotify account below! Once the connection is made, you will be redirected to a new page and will see your username on the top right. Press the start button to select a song. This will open the first input box, where you can start guessing!
