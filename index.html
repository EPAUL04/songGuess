Guess My Song

Welcome!

This is a web game designed to pull a playlist from your spotify library, then get a random song from it. You will be given a series of clues to guess which song has been selected.


How to play:

First, hit the login button at the bottom of the page. This will open a login page to connect to your Spotify account. Once you log in, you will be redirected to the main webpage. Once on this page, make sure not to refresh the browser or your data may be lost and you will have to log back in.


Once you're on the game page, hit the start button to select a song. At this point, the first input box on the left side of the screen will become available and you may begin guessing. Clues will be shown on the right side of the screen. They will appear when the corresponding guess becomes available, or whenever a song that you guessed shares traits with the selected song. Note: when you guess a song, whatever you type is passed as a search query to Spotify, and the track is fetched to compare to the answer. This means that, while no songs will pop up as options while you type, it still doesn't have to be a perfect match to get the correct answer!


Clues are given as follows:

  1. a playlist you've added the song to
  2. when the song was first added to your library
  3. the artist(s)
  4. the album
  5. no extra clues!
  6. the first letter of the song's title
  7. no extra clues!




More detail on how this website interacts with your data:

This website interacts with the Spotify API. First, it follows the steps listed in this guide to direct the user through the authentication process and the process to fetch information from the user's profile. Next, it sends a series of requests through the API to acquire your library of playlists (to pick a playlist to pull a song from), to access the specified playlist, to access a random song from that playlist, and to access the actual listing of the song (to get features about the song such as album and artist information).


What are the security concerns?

Your data is accessed through Spotify's API, which follows its own security procedures. Your username and library information are never stored in the cache, local browser storage, or the script associated with this website. The only information stored in local storage is the song name and website information (client code and redirect URL). This means that the website itself may be slightly insecure, but your data itself is secured by Spotify's API security.


You can find more information on Spotify's data policies and protections here.
